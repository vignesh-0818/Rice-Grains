Frequently Asked Questions by Wholesale Buyers

Key operational details regarding order tonnages, payment structures, quality testing, and terminal loading.

Our baseline Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) is 1 Metric Ton (1,000 kg), which equals 20 sacks of 50kg or 40 sacks of 25kg, suitable for independent supermarkets and specialty distributors. For optimal freight rates, we recommend full truckloads (FTL) of 20 to 25 Metric Tons or standard 20ft maritime export sea containers holding 26 Metric Tons (520 sacks).

For international container dispatches, we accept 100% Irrevocable Documentary Letters of Credit (L/C at Sight) issued by recognized international commercial banks, or advance telegraphic wire transfer (T/T). For established domestic supermarket chains and institutional caterers, we offer structured 30/60-day credit lines backed by revolving bank guarantees.

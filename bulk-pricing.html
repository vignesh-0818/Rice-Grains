Purchasing Arrangements

Flexible Wholesale Contract Models

Whether your procurement cycle requires spot truckload dispatches or annual price hedging, we accommodate structured trading agreements.

1. Spot Market Buying

Execute fast-turnaround truckload orders at current daily mill-gate pricing. Ideal for distributors taking advantage of seasonal harvest price drops.

Lead Time: 24-48 hours dispatch from standing terminal inventory.

2. 30/60-Day Forward Price Lock

Lock in a fixed price per Metric Ton for up to 60 days with guaranteed milling reservation. Protects supermarket margins from unexpected mid-season inflation.

Commitment: Minimum 50 MT volume commitment with refundable booking deposit.

3. Annual Institutional Supply Agreement

Scheduled monthly or bi-weekly truckload dispatches under unified quality specifications. Preferred by large hotel chains, food caterers, and export houses.

Terms: Revolving Letter of Credit (LC) and priority milling queue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding wholesale grain procurement, sampling, payment terms, and logistics.

Our standard Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) is 1 Metric Ton (1,000 kg), which equals 20 bags of 50kg or 40 bags of 25kg. For trial lots and initial lab verification, sample 500g parcels are dispatched via courier to verified commercial entities upon request.

Every lot passes through multi-camera bichromatic optical sorters that achieve 100% sortex purity by removing foreign seeds, chalky grains, and discolored kernels. Each dispatch is accompanied by an internal lab certificate confirming moisture content (max 12.5% for Basmati), average grain length, and broken percentage. Third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS/Cotecna) is available upon request.

For domestic wholesale contracts, we accept Wire Transfer (RTGS/NEFT/ACH) with advance deposit against proforma and balance upon weighbridge dispatch slip. For export consignments, we accommodate Irrevocable Letters of Credit (LC at sight) issued by prime international banks, or Telegraphic Transfer (TT: 30% advance, 70% against Bill of Lading scan).

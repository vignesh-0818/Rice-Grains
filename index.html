Contract Execution Workflow

How Our Wholesale Process Works

From initial sample courier to multi-trailer dispatch, our four-step execution guarantees transparency.

1

1. Inquire & Specs

Submit requested grain varieties, volume requirement (MT), sack packaging preference, and destination location.

2

2. Sample & Lab QA

We dispatch a sealed 500g lot sample with official digital moisture and elongation certificates for buyer approval.

3

3. Contract Execution

Formal commercial proforma issued with agreed pricing, delivery schedule, payment terms (LC/TT), and packaging artwork.

4

4. Multi-Modal Dispatch

Loading on dedicated 25-ton trailers or maritime container stuffing at port with weighbridge slips and e-way transit tracking.
